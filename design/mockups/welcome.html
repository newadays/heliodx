What to expect

Five steps. You decide at every one.

Studies are analyzed on arrival, so a draft is usually waiting when you open one. Every agent step is logged beside your own actions.

Analyze

Findings draft the moment a study arrives and appear as they are detected.

02

Review

03

Sign

Plain language, written from the signed report only and checked against it.
